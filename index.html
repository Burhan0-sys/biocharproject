The Environmental Damage

The Devastating Impact of Galamsey

Unregulated illegal gold mining damages natural ecosystems, destroying topsoil and creating toxic hazards for local communities.

Heavy Metal Toxicity

The widespread use of mercury leaves behind toxic residues that leach into ground soils, rendering farmland unusable and posing severe health risks to nearby settlements.

Extreme Soil Acidity

Excavation exposes deep minerals to air and water, creating Acid Mine Drainage. Soil pH drops sharply below 4.0, inhibiting plant life and crop growth.

Nutrient Depletion

Heavy machinery removes fertile topsoil layers, stripping vital macro-nutrients—Nitrogen (N), Phosphorus (P), and Potassium (K)—necessary for agriculture.

Our Reclamation Approach

Combining Low-Cost Biochar with Autonomous Cloud Sensing

BioChar-Guard is a multi-stage land reclamation platform. We convert agricultural residues—such as discarded coconut husks and cocoa pods—into highly porous, alkaline biochar through controlled pyrolysis.

When applied to degraded mine tailings, the biochar neutralizes acidity, traps heavy metal ions, and retains moisture. Field-deployed ESP32 sensor stakes then monitor soil health recovery in real time.

End-to-End Workflow

How BioChar-Guard Works

From waste collection to remote telemetry verification in four structured steps.

01

Waste Collection

Coconut shells and crop waste are collected from local farms, turning agricultural waste into resources.

02

Pyrolysis & Soil Fix

Biomass undergoes oxygen-limited pyrolysis into porous biochar, applied directly to neutralize toxic mine soil.

03

IoT Field Stakes

Solar-powered ESP32 stakes continuously record soil pH and NPK nutrient values on-site.

04

Portal Monitoring

Field data streams to the online dashboard, giving researchers real-time recovery metrics.
